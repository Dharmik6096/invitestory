Frequently Asked Questions

How do guests open the wedding invitation? +
Guests simply click the link you send them on WhatsApp, SMS, or Email. It opens instantly on any phone browser (iPhone or Android) with zero app downloads required.
Can I add my own couple photos and songs? +
Yes! You can upload your own photos into the story polaroids and pick from our royal Shehnai tracks or upload your custom MP3 song.
How long does my invitation stay live online? +
Every invitation is hosted on super-fast cloud infrastructure for a full 1 year after your wedding date so guests can revisit memories and photos anytime.
