# Papercut Magenta (Lotus) - Wedding Invitation Card

An exact, 1:1 replica of the **Papercut Magenta** interactive digital wedding invitation card from Balcony (`https://www.itsbalcony.com/templates/papercut-magenta/index.html?preview=1&trial=1`).

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Elements

- **Lotus Papercut Cover Opening**:
  - Tap or click "Open the Invitation" to trigger the layered papercut arch opening animation.
  - Ornate magenta and gold arches part to reveal the couple and holy pond.
- **Background Music**:
  - Plays the royal track *Jashn-e-bahara (Instrumental)* with floating top-right play/mute control.
- **Sanskrit Invocation**:
  - Lord Ganesha emblem and sacred mantra (*श्री गणेशाय नमः*).
- **Couple & Family Scrolls**:
  - Groom's family card & Bride's family card with gotra, parents, and grandparents.
- **Papercut Date Reveal Mechanism**:
  - Click "Reveal the Date!" to trigger rotating floral blooms and rotating date dial settling on the wedding date.
- **Photo Carousel & Keepsake Stories**:
  - Multiple photos with carousel pagination arrows and dots.
  - Heartwarming keepsake story notes.
- **Interactive Ceremonies Carousel**:
  - Arched cards for each ceremony: **Ring Ceremony**, **Mehendi**, **Haldi**, **Sangeet**, **Vivaah**, and **Reception**.
  - Venue location, timings, and direct Google Maps links.
- **Compliments & Notes**:
  - "With Best Compliments From" relatives card.
  - "A Note From Us" card with guest instructions.
- **Social Popups & Interactive RSVP**:
  - Instagram, Venue Map, and Add to Calendar (.ics) popups.
  - Shared Balcony RSVP module with event answers, headcount, and wishes.

---

## 🚀 How to View & Customize

### Viewing:
- **Platform Version**: `card 2/index.html`
- **Standalone Version**: `card 2/standalone.html` (double-click to open in any browser)

### Customizing:
Open **`card 2/config.js`** to change:
- `brideName` & `groomName`
- `eventISO` (Date)
- `groomFamily` & `brideFamily`
- `ceremonies` (dates, times, venues, map links)
- `gallery` (photos and notes)
- `compliments` & `note`
